# Lessons to paste into your Skool classroom

These replace the GoHighLevel "claim your funnel account" lesson from the sprint
kit. Paste each one into a Skool lesson. Swap the bracketed parts.

---

## Lesson: Claim your funnel account (Day 0)

Your funnel account is ready for you. It's included with your membership.

**Your code this month:** [MONTH CODE]

1. Open this link: [APP_URL]/signup?code=[MONTH CODE]
2. Use the same email as your Skool account.
3. Answer 6 quick questions. The AI builds your landing page, free guide, 3 emails, ads and Skool About page.
4. Click Publish. Your page is live.

Your funnel account stays active while you're a member. Each month, a new code
appears in this lesson. Enter it in Settings when the app asks.

## Lesson: Turn on tracking (Day 3)

1. In Meta Events Manager, copy your Pixel (dataset) ID.
2. Click Settings › Generate access token. Copy it.
3. In your funnel account: Settings › Meta Pixel & Conversions API. Paste both. Save.
4. Open your landing page and submit a test lead.
5. Check Events Manager › Test events. You see PageView and Lead.

Your Lead event fires from the browser and from our server. Meta matches them, so
tracking keeps working with ad blockers.

---

## Lesson: Get your custom ad plan (Day 6)

1. Open Ads Manager. Set the date to the last 3 days.
2. Copy spend, impressions, link clicks and leads.
3. In your funnel account, open **Ad review** and paste the numbers.
4. You get a plan: what to keep, what to fix and your next daily budget.
5. Post your plan in 🎯 Ad Review. I'll add my notes.
